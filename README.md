# 🚲 Bike Share Demand Forecasting

**Course:** MBA Business Data Analytics  
**Tools:** R · ggplot2 · dplyr · regression modeling  
**Dataset:** Bike share demand data with weather, seasonal, and temporal variables

---

## Business Problem

How can bike rental demand be predicted using weather and time-based factors?  
This project uses regression analysis to estimate daily bike rental volume and understand how environmental and calendar variables influence customer demand.

---

## What I Did

**1. Data Preparation**  
- Loaded and explored the bike share dataset in R
- Reviewed variable structure, summary statistics, and relationships among demand drivers
- Prepared predictors related to temperature, humidity, windspeed, season, holiday effects, and working-day patterns
- Investigated how ridership changed across weather and time conditions

**2. Regression Modeling**  
- Built regression models to predict bike rental demand
- Evaluated coefficient significance and overall model fit
- Compared how weather and calendar variables contributed to forecast accuracy
- Interpreted results to identify the strongest predictors of demand

**3. Demand Interpretation**  
- Assessed the role of temperature, seasonality, and working-day behavior in daily rentals
- Examined how adverse weather conditions reduced demand
- Translated model findings into operational forecasting insight

---

## Key Insight

Bike rental demand was strongly influenced by temperature, seasonality, and working-day patterns, while unfavorable weather conditions reduced ridership. The analysis showed how demand forecasting can support better operational and staffing decisions.

---

## Business Value

- Demonstrated how regression can support short-term demand forecasting
- Identified the environmental and calendar factors most associated with bike rentals
- Showed how analytics can improve planning for transportation services
- Reinforced the business value of predictive demand modeling
